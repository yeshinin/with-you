For health systems, ACOs, and health plans

Discharge plans assume households can carry them out. Many can’t, and you pay for it.

With You sets up the care plan around the household: who does each task, in what language, their learning preference and at what times they can answer. It calls when each task or appointment is due and walks them through it by voice, or with visual aids where needed, adjusting as the household’s situation changes. With You flags barriers before care is missed, routes concerns to the right clinical or community support, and uses patterns across households to identify risks earlier and target support more precisely.

Our goal:

  • Patients and care partners have the skills, confidence, and the right support to carry out their care plan.
  • More prescriptions filled and medicines taken as prescribed.
  • More follow-up visits and labs completed on time.
  • Barriers addressed before care is missed.
  • Fewer avoidable ER visits and readmissions.
  • Less coordination work for care teams.
  • More eligible care reimbursed and lower avoidable spending.

How it works

  1. A nurse approves the plan in about two minutes.
  2. Fitted to the household: who does each task, in what language, and when.
  3. A call when each task is due walks them through it. No app needed.
  4. Barriers go to a community health worker; clinical questions go to your team. Every issue tracked until it’s closed.
  • No new dashboard
  • Practical barriers don’t reach nurses
  • Connects to Epic through SMART on FHIR

With the patient’s permission, With You connects to Epic using SMART on FHIR and reads their medications, appointments, care plans, and clinical notes. Discharge instructions reach the household without anyone re-entering them.

What the evidence says

  • About one-third fewer hospital days with community health worker support after discharge.

    Source: Vasan et al., Health Services Research, 2020

  • 30-day readmissions fell from 11.9% to 8.3% with transition coaching for patients and caregivers.

    Source: Coleman et al., Archives of Internal Medicine, 2006

Approaches like this have been shown to work when delivered by people. With You is designed to reach more households than staff alone can.
