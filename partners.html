For health systems, ACOs, and health plans

Discharge plans assume households can carry them out. Many can’t, and you pay for it.

With You builds guidance around what each household can realistically manage, their skills, energy, time, and resources, so patients and care partners can put the plan into practice, with no added work for your nurses.

The scale of the need

Millions of patients go home each year to carry out a care plan. Billions are spent when that plan breaks down.

  • 3.8 million 30-day adult readmissions a year. Source: HCUP Statistical Brief #278 (2018 data)
  • ~$58 billion est. Their annual cost, at $15,200 each. Source: derived from HCUP Statistical Brief #278
  • 40% Of hospital returns after discharge are ER visits without admission. Source: Vashi et al., JAMA, 2013
  • 22% Of family caregivers have been trained for the medical tasks they do at home. Source: AARP & NAC, Caregiving in the US 2025

The gap is widening

The care load on households is growing. The capacity to carry it isn’t.

Capacity is stretched

  • 22% Of family caregivers have been trained for the medical tasks they do at home. Source: AARP & NAC, 2025
  • 1 in 5 Caregivers report fair or poor health caused by caregiving. Source: AARP & NAC, 2025
  • 1 in 4 Caregivers feel socially isolated. Source: AARP & NAC, 2025

Discharge plans assume households can absorb this. With You is built around what they can realistically manage.

Where it pays

  • Hospitals

    You’re accountable for:
    Medicare readmission penalties (up to 3% of base payments)7, TEAM 30-day episode costs8, fixed transplant payments, bed capacity, no-shows.
    With You moves:
    Readmissions, follow-up visits kept, bed days, staff time.

  • ACOs and value-based groups

    You’re accountable for:
    Total cost of care and shared savings.

    ER visits, readmissions, avoidable spending.

  • Medicare Advantage and health plans

    You’re accountable for:
    Star Ratings, including readmissions, transitions of care, follow-up after ER visits, and medication adherence.
    With You moves:
    Follow-up visits, prescriptions filled, readmissions.

  • Kidney care organizations

    You’re accountable for:
    Hospitalization costs for patients on dialysis and after transplant.
    With You moves:
    Hospital and ER use.

How it works

  1. A nurse approves the plan in about two minutes.
  2. Fitted to the household: who does each task, in what language, and when.
  3. A call when each task is due walks them through it. No app needed.
  4. Barriers go to a community health worker; clinical questions go to your team. Every issue tracked until it’s closed.
  • No new dashboard
  • Practical barriers don’t reach nurses
  • EHR integration after the pilot proves value

What the evidence says

  • About one-third fewer hospital days with community health worker support after discharge.

    Source: Vasan et al., Health Services Research, 2020

  • 30-day readmissions fell from 11.9% to 8.3% with transition coaching for patients and caregivers.

    Source: Coleman et al., Archives of Internal Medicine, 2006

Approaches like this have been shown to work when delivered by people. With You is designed to reach more households than staff alone can.
