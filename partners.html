For health systems and partners

Discharge transfers responsibility, not capability.

The plan is usually right. What fails is whether the household can carry it out, and those failures come back as readmissions and ER visits.

The cost

  1. $15,200 is the average cost of a hospital readmission.1
  2. 1 in 5 patients have an adverse event after discharge.2
  3. 40% of post-discharge hospital returns are ER visits without admission.3

How it fits your team

  • No new dashboardIssues arrive where staff already work, with context gathered.
  • Two-minute nurse approvalA nurse approves the plan before discharge.
  • Phased integrationThe pilot starts with a simple staff screen; EHR integration follows.

What the evidence says

Approaches like this have been shown to work. Community health worker support after discharge cut total hospital days by about a third across three trials.4 Coaching patients and care partners through the transition lowered 30-day readmissions from 11.9% to 8.3%.5

What we’d measure in a 90-day pilot

  • Problems caught and closed
  • Questions answered at visits
  • Follow-up visits kept
  • Daily tasks confirmed
  • Household confidence (CTM-3)6
  • No added nurse workload

ED visits and readmissions tracked as early signals.

Where we are

We’ve completed discovery interviews with patients, care partners, and clinicians and are building our first prototype. We’re looking for a health system or kidney care partner for a first pilot.

Why this gets better over time

Hospitals already predict who will be readmitted. With You learns what goes wrong at home, and when, from the households themselves: data that grows with every family it supports.
