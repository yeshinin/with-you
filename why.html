Why I’m building this

I’m Yeshi Dolma. My path towards improving public health is not a linear academic choice. It is shaped by over a decade of navigating healthcare systems as a primary caregiver where I became an informed advocate and observed how consistently outcomes are shaped by access, care processes, and structural inequities. Over the last few years, I’ve been thinking more at the systems level, what patterns keep repeating, data biases that leave the most vulnerable population behind and where the right technology could actually move the needle.

Professionally, I bring a decade of experience as an Applied ML Scientist at big tech, building and scaling predictive machine learning models and causal inference.

As a family caregiver for eight years, caring for my parent through cancer, a liver transplant, and post-transplant complications, I understand the love, responsibility, and exhaustion that caregiving can hold. I learned how much happens between medical appointments and how much families must figure out themselves.

I’m building With You to help patients and caregivers understand their care, navigate everyday life, and feel supported as they find their own new normal in their complex care journey. Looking for help when you’re in pain, frightened, or overwhelmed asks something very different of you than using any random app on an ordinary day. I’m building With You around that reality: to make care easier to understand and everyday life more manageable, with support that meets patients and caregivers where they are.

It’s an audacious dream, but I’m excited to work towards better public-health outcomes.

Without health security, human potential is unfairly constrained; equitable health must come first so anyone can dream.

Yeshi Dolma · Founder
