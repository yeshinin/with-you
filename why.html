Why I’m building this

Rooted in lived caregiving experience. Shaped by hundreds of patient and care partner stories.

I’m Yeshi Dolma, an applied machine learning scientist and family care partner. For nine years at Microsoft, I built predictive models for predicting user engagement and built systems serving billions of interactions worldwide. My work involved causal inference, diverse user contexts, and building with sparse, noisy, biased data.

For eight years, I was also my parent’s primary caregiver through HCC, a liver transplant, and post-transplant complications. I know the love, responsibility, and exhaustion caregiving can hold, and how much families must figure out between appointments.

A person in pain or a care partner running on little sleep has less attention and energy to spare. Their support should account for that.

I’m building With You to bring guidance into the moment care needs to happen: help with the next step, in a form someone can use, and human support when instructions alone aren’t enough. The goal is to make complex care at home more manageable and help families build confidence without leaving them to figure everything out alone. There should never be a silent break in recovery.
