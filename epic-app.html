With You Explorer

A read-only test app that connects to Epic’s sandbox to explore how a discharge plan can become step-by-step guidance at home. It uses Epic’s fictional test patients only.

Last updated 17 November 2026

What the app does

The explorer signs in to Epic’s sandbox using SMART on FHIR and reads a test patient’s record. It uses that record to draft a plan for home: which tasks are due, when, and what each step involves.

It is for testing and design only. It is not used with real patients or in anyone’s care.

What data it reads, and why

Patient basics
Name, date of birth, and preferred language, to address the patient and choose the language for guidance.
Medications
Medication orders and the medicines they refer to, to build the list of medicine tasks with dose and timing.
Conditions and allergies
The problem list, diagnoses, health concerns, and allergies, to give context for the plan, such as which warning signs to watch for.
Hospital stays and visits
Encounters, to find the most recent hospital stay and its discharge date.
Appointments and care plans
Follow-up visits and care-plan activities, to schedule reminders and walk through each task.
Clinical notes
The list of notes and the text of each note, to see which discharge instructions exist only as written text.
Optional, if enabled
Lab results and vital signs, procedures, goals, orders and referrals, care team, and immunizations, to see what else could support care at home.

The explorer asks Epic only for the data above, and only for the test patient chosen at sign-in. It saves each response on the computer running it, to compare what arrives as structured data with what appears only in notes.

Read-only

The explorer only reads. It never creates, changes, or deletes anything in Epic.

Anything it reads is kept on the computer running the explorer, never on a shared server. See the privacy policy.

How to disconnect

Choose Disconnect in the explorer. This ends the Epic session and deletes the access token and every record the explorer saved on that computer.

Access also ends on its own when the Epic sign-in expires.
